# Store 3 — direction artistique

Le paysage `garden.png` a été généré avec l'outil intégré imagegen, à partir de la référence visuelle fournie par l'utilisateur. Le Mac, les interfaces, le câble et les pétales sont des éléments interactifs dessinés en HTML/CSS/canvas.

Prompt final :

> Create a premium finely crafted PIXEL ART game environment background for a full-screen interactive product website. Widescreen 16:9, ideally 1536x864. Use the attached reference only for visual style, palette, density and composition, not its UI or devices. Scene: tranquil Japanese hillside garden at sunset, large distant snowcapped Mount Fuji in the middle, deep violet/lavender sky transitioning to luminous salmon peach at horizon, tiny soft pixel clouds. A layered orange-red pagoda and small torii gate at left midground, warm illuminated tiny windows. Rich grass and wildflowers across the lower third. A massive dark cherry blossom tree frames the RIGHT edge, its detailed pink coral blossom canopy stretches over the upper right quarter. Small cherry tree left edge. Distant mountain ridges in muted blue violet. Foreground grasses and little white and pink flower pixels. Right-center lower-midground MUST have a calm open grassy space with no devices or props, where a live interactive laptop will be overlaid. Upper LEFT 45% is mostly calm low-detail mauve sky suitable for white heading text overlay, do not place clouds or branches there. Strict intentional retro 16-bit PIXEL ART, visible crisp square pixels, densely hand-dithered color clusters, no smooth gradients, no anti-aliased vector edges, no painting brush strokes, no photorealism, no blur. Deep atmospheric layered composition, nostalgic cozy Japanese adventure game aesthetic. Absolutely no text, no letters, no UI, no laptop, no computer, no logo, no watermark. The final is just the landscape background asset.
